# Art direction

A small handmade toy forest, with warm cream animals, sage green foliage, soft terracotta, honey yellow and muted blue. The rounded tree silhouettes and animal proportions were created specifically for Elenita. No existing children's franchise is imitated.

The ground is continuous. Clearings form a ring and share visible connecting paths. The open foreground gives touch targets room; trees frame the back of each clearing. Soft shadows and distance fog separate the active clearing from its neighbors. The responsive camera adjusts its distance, and fog follows that distance so portrait views remain visible.

The child sees a tiny leaf brand, sound/time controls, an adult gate, and an illustrated trail. Text is optional support: all essential actions have recognizable pictures or physical objects. There are no success badges, counters, rewards, or negative feedback.

Night uses blue-green light with enough ambient illumination to keep animals and shapes readable. No flashes, camera shake, or high-frequency brightness changes are used. Reduced motion stops idle movement and camera travel.
